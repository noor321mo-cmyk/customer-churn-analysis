# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns to identify the key factors that influence customers to leave a telecom service.

The analysis focuses on contract types, payment methods, customer tenure, internet service type, and senior citizen status to generate actionable customer retention insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify the major factors associated with customer churn.
- Analyze churn rates across different customer segments.
- Compare churn behavior based on contract types and payment methods.
- Understand the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python**
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
- **Seaborn** – Statistical visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis and documentation

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 📄 Contract Type

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate.

| Contract Type | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-Month | **42%** |
| One Year | **11%** |
| Two Year | **3%** |

Longer-term contracts are associated with substantially lower churn.

### 💳 Payment Method

Electronic check users show the highest churn rate.

| Payment Method | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Electronic Check | **45%** |
| Other payment methods | **~15–18%** |

This indicates that payment method is an important factor to investigate when designing retention strategies.

### ⏳ Customer Tenure

Churn is highest among newer customers.

| Tenure | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Less than 1 Year | **50%** |
| 1–3 Years | **35%** |
| More than 3 Years | **15%** |

The results highlight the importance of customer engagement during the first year.

### 🌐 Internet Service

| Internet Service | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Fiber Optic | **30%** |
| DSL | **20%** |

Fiber-optic customers show a higher churn rate than DSL customers.

### 👥 Senior Citizens

Senior citizens show a higher churn rate than non-senior customers.

| Customer Group | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Senior Citizens | **41%** |
| Non-Senior Citizens | **26%** |

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Promote Long-Term Contracts**  
   Provide incentives and benefits for customers who move from month-to-month contracts to yearly or two-year plans.

2. **Address Electronic Check Churn**  
   Encourage customers using electronic checks to adopt alternative payment methods.

3. **Focus on New Customers**  
   Introduce stronger onboarding, engagement, and loyalty programs during the first year.

4. **Improve Fiber Customer Experience**  
   Investigate service quality, pricing, reliability, and customer satisfaction among fiber-optic users.

5. **Create Targeted Senior Customer Programs**  
   Provide personalized support and retention offers for senior customers.

## 📈 Conclusion

The analysis shows that **contract type, payment method, and customer tenure** are important factors associated with customer churn.

The highest-risk groups identified in this analysis include:

- Month-to-month customers
- Electronic check users
- Customers with less than one year of tenure
- Fiber-optic customers
- Senior citizens

Targeted retention strategies for these groups can help businesses reduce churn and improve customer loyalty.
